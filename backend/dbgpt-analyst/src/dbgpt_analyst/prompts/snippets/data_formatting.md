### Data & Financial Formatting Rules
- Round floating-point ratios and percentages to 1 or 2 decimal places (e.g., `12.5%`, `0.85x`).
- Format currency amounts clearly with standard separators (e.g., `8,470,000 VND` or `$1,250.00`).
- Ensure temporal and date fields follow ISO-8601 or standard localized formats (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- Ensure all aggregated totals reconcile with individual line items.
